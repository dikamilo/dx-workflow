# `brainstorm.md` — shape

Written at the container root, beside `research/`, `frame.md`, and `diagnosis.md`. It carries what the conversation rejected and why building nothing lost — the part no other artifact has a home for.

```markdown
---
created: <today>
---

# Brainstorm — <id>

## The question
<the problem restated without the proposed solution>

## Alternatives weighed
<each option, why it lost or won — including the priced do-nothing and what it cost>

## Conclusion & route
<the chosen outcome, the ramp taken, the bundling test's verdict if it ran, and what the
divergence and the adversarial pass left standing — riskiest assumption, what breaks if
this succeeds, any caveat>

## Resolved unknowns
| Question | Answer |

## Not doing
<explicitly rejected scope, so it isn't relitigated downstream>
```

Write `none` under a heading rather than deleting it.

**Resolved unknowns is the self-check.** An empty table means nothing was settled, so the route isn't earned: keep talking, or downgrade the ramp.

`dx-plan`, `dx-frame`, and `dx-roadmap` read this as settled context — write each section to survive being read cold by someone who wasn't in the conversation.
